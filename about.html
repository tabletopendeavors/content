About TableTop Endeavors

I am a hobbyist often of, but not limited to, the tabletop variety. My name is, Chris and I am master of nothing but enthusiast of just about anything. If there’s a way to be creative, I’m likely to not just dabble in it, but strive to be maybe sort of good at it. As I learn and grow, I look to share my experience. Sometimes I even make videos about it! If nothing else, I hope to teach others what (not) to do in their own endeavors.

While my current interests are focused in the tabletop space, including war games, board games, and role-play games — these will make up the bulk of what I share online — I’ve dabbled in a great many things, including fiction and music. My love of these other creative endeavors may slip in from time to time.

My videos largely focus on techniques and methods new to me. I avoid taking an authoritative stance since I am also still learning, but that doesn’t mean we can’t learn together. I am always open to trying new materials, tools, methods, and more, so if you have a suggestion for something you’d like me to try on my channel, feel free to contact me via email.
