The Rookie Mistakes I Made | Elegoo Mars 3

I state that curing prints is optional. This is simply not true, and you must cure all prints. Handling uncured resin will eventually lead to the development of an allergy to resin, and once you have this allergy, it will never go away.

Having resin cure and stick to the FEP does not necessarily require replacement of the FEP unless it was also damaged. In most cases you can simple apply some pressure to the FEP from underneath and pop off the resin. Most printers also have a “tank clean” feature to assist in this.
